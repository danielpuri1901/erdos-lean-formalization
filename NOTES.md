# witt24: S(5, 8, 24) exists

## Argument

- Points: `Fin 24`. Blocks: the 759 octads (weight-8 words of the extended binary Golay code),
  generated by `scripts/octads.py` from the cyclic [23,12] Golay code with generator
  polynomial g(x) = x^11 + x^10 + x^6 + x^5 + x^4 + x^2 + 1, extended by a parity bit.
  Stored in Lean as 24-bit `Nat` masks; `toFS m = univ.filter (fun i => m.testBit i)`.
- Facts checked by the kernel on the masks:
  1. every octad has popcount 8 (`pc 24 m = 8`);
  2. for every pair of positions i < j in the list, `pc 24 (a &&& b) ≤ 4`
     (287,661 pairs, split into chunks of rows in separate modules).
- Bridge: `(toFS m).card = pc 24 m` (induction on the fuel with `Fin.sum_univ_succ`,
  `Nat.testBit_succ`), and `toFS (a &&& b) = toFS a ∩ toFS b`.
- Consequences: the 759 Finsets are pairwise distinct (two equal ones would meet in 8 points),
  so `blocks.card = 759`; distinct blocks meet in ≤ 4 points.
- Counting: a 5-set lies in at most one block (two blocks containing it meet in ≥ 5 points).
  `U = blocks.biUnion (powersetCard 5)` is a disjoint union, so `|U| = 759 * 56 = 42504 = C(24,5)`,
  and `U ⊆ univ.powersetCard 5`, hence `U = univ.powersetCard 5`: every 5-set lies in some block.
  Together: the filter has exactly one element.
